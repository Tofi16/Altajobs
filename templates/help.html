Frequently asked questions

How do I withdraw funds?
Go to Wallet → Withdraw and follow the on-screen steps. Withdrawals are typically reviewed within 24 hours.

How do I report a listing or user?
Use the flag icon on any marketplace listing, or the report option on a profile, to send it to our moderation team.

How do I become verified?
Visit Settings → Get Verified to purchase a verification badge and boost trust with buyers and clients.
